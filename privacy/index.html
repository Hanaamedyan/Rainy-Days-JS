Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information this Rainy Days demo store collects and how it is used. This site was built as a student e-commerce project; it does not have a real backend and does not send your data to any company.

1. Information We Collect

The checkout form on this site collects contact, shipping, and payment-style details (name, email, phone, address, and card fields) purely to simulate a real checkout flow. This information is never transmitted anywhere — it is validated in your browser and then discarded; it is not stored, logged, or sent to a server.

2. Local Storage

To let your shopping basket persist as you move between pages, this site stores your cart contents (product id, title, price, size, and quantity) in your browser's localStorage. This data stays on your device and is only ever read by this site. Clearing your browser data will remove it.

A simulated order summary (order number, items, and total) is temporarily stored in your browser's sessionStorage so the confirmation page can display it. This is cleared automatically when you close the browser tab.

3. Third-Party Services

Product data (titles, descriptions, prices, images) is loaded from the Rainy Days API, hosted by Noroff, each time you browse the shop. Font Awesome icons are loaded from a public content delivery network. No personal data is sent to either service as part of normal browsing.

4. Cookies

This site does not use cookies for tracking or advertising.

5. Children's Privacy

This is a course project and is not intended to collect data from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the project evolves. Any changes will be posted on this page.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed through the contact details listed in the site footer.
